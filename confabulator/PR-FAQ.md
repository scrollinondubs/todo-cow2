# Moo-ving Tasks Forward: Introducing Todo Cow

## The one-handed app for herdsmen to track and manage tasks per cow efficiently

Today, we are thrilled to announce the launch of Todo Cow, a groundbreaking web app designed specifically for herdsmen in small to medium-sized dairy farms. Todo Cow revolutionizes task management by allowing herdsmen to track individual cow tasks with ease and precision, ensuring no task is forgotten and every cow gets the care it needs. This innovative app tackles the persistent issue of lost task records, which often results in health issues or compliance gaps, by providing a digital solution that fits right in the palm of your hand.

### The Problem

In dairy farms with herds ranging from twenty to two hundred cows, herdsmen rely heavily on memory or outdated methods like whiteboards and paper diaries to track tasks for each cow. This method is not only cumbersome but also prone to errors and omissions, as the record-keeping tools are often not where the herdsman is when they remember a task. The consequences of these oversights can be significant, leading to missed vaccinations, delayed hoof checks, or other health-related issues that may not surface until much later. Compliance with regulations also suffers, as there is no durable record of what has been done and when, complicating inspections and audits.

### The Solution

Todo Cow provides a simple yet effective solution to this long-standing problem. Designed to be used one-handed on a smartphone, Todo Cow allows herdsmen to add tasks for individual cows with just a few taps. Each task has a title, a due date, and a completion status, ensuring that no detail is overlooked. The app's herd view feature provides an at-a-glance overview of which cows have outstanding tasks, prioritizing those with overdue work. This straightforward approach is tailored explicitly for the herdsman, providing a digital record that is accessible wherever they are, ensuring better animal care and regulatory compliance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions

**Q: What is Todo Cow?**  
A: Todo Cow is a streamlined web app that allows herdsmen to track and manage tasks for each cow in their herd on a single screen.

**Q: Who is Todo Cow for?**  
A: Todo Cow is designed for herdsmen managing small to medium-sized dairy farms, typically with 20 to 200 cows.

**Q: What makes Todo Cow different from existing solutions?**  
A: Unlike complex herd management software, Todo Cow focuses solely on task management per cow, is simple to use, and fits entirely on a smartphone screen for use on the go.

**Q: How much does Todo Cow cost?**  
A: Todo Cow is currently free for use, as we focus on building a strong community and gathering user feedback.

### Product Questions

**Q: What are the key features?**  
A: Key features include adding cows with names and ear tags, assigning tasks to specific cows with due dates, marking tasks as done, and a herd view that shows cows with outstanding tasks.

**Q: What platforms does Todo Cow support?**  
A: Todo Cow is a responsive web app, optimized for use on smartphones and accessible through any web browser.

**Q: How do I get started?**  
A: Visit our website on your smartphone, sign up, and start adding your cows and their tasks right away.

### Business Questions

**Q: What is the business model?**  
A: Todo Cow is currently focused on user adoption and feedback. Future monetization strategies will be considered based on user needs and feedback.

**Q: What are the future plans for Todo Cow?**  
A: Our vision is to maintain the simplicity of the app while exploring potential integrations that could enhance user experience without adding complexity.

### Technical Questions

**Q: How does Todo Cow work technically?**  
A: Todo Cow is built using Next.js with TypeScript and a SQL database for data persistence, deployed on Vercel for optimal performance and reliability.

**Q: How do you ensure security/privacy/reliability?**  
A: We prioritize user data security and privacy by employing industry-standard encryption and secure data storage practices. Regular updates and maintenance ensure the app's reliability.
